Contains all 48 teams mapped to groups A to L. - Team stats: Offense Rating (1-99), Defense Rating (1-99), Midfield/Control Rating (1-99), Star Player Name, and Flag Emoji. 2. **Simulation Algorithm**: - Uses Poisson-like probability distribution or weighted random goals based on Offense vs. opposing Defense ratings. - Standard Match: Generates score `G1 : G2` and generates text-based event commentary (goals, yellow/red cards, injury time) for selected matches. - Knockout Stage: Handles Extra Time (30 mins) and Penalty Shootouts if tied. 3. **Group Stage Resolution**: - Computes standings: Points, Goal Difference, Goals Scored, Head-to-Head, and Fair Play. - Computes Best 3rd-Place Ranking: Compiles the 3rd-place teams from all 12 groups, ranks them, and takes the top 8 to advance. 4. **Knockout Bracket Generation**: - Seeds the Round of 32 dynamically based on official FIFA scheduling logic (e.g., Group winners vs 3rd-place or runners-up). - Allows users to click on a matchup to manually pick a winner, or click "Quick Simulate Match", or "Simulate All" for the round. --- ### Component Details #### [NEW] [index.html](file:///C:/Users/User/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/fifa-2026-predictor/index.html) -
